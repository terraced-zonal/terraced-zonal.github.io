A Linear-Time Construction on 𝕊2 Achieving a Sublinear Energy Gap on Smale’s Seventh Problem

Four published constructions and TerracedZonal, with every point's Voronoi cell colored by how far it is from a regular hexagon. The sphere turns slowly; the framed panel below each sphere is an equal-area zoom of the boxed region at the center of view, scaled to unit area per point. At the far right, TZ-R shows how far numerical optimization started from TerracedZonal gets. Each card also gives the configuration's energy gap per point above the asymptotic optimum1, with the lowest of the five constructions at each N marked.

  1. Energy gap. E = ∑i<j log(1/‖xi − xj‖), each pair counted once. Cards show the gap per point (E − EBHS)/N above the Brauchart–Hardin–Saff asymptotic energy EBHS; lower is better, and dividing by N makes slightly different N comparable. Below it: E itself, summed exactly over all pairs. Paper: §1 and App. A.1.
  2. Perimeter excess P/Phex − 1, where Phex is the perimeter of the regular spherical hexagon of the same area: 0 for a perfect cell, at least 2.4% for any pentagon. A geometric diagnostic, not an energy bound; cards show its mean and the share of cells above 1%. Paper: Fig. 1 and App. B.1.
  3. Cell anisotropy √(λmax/λmin) − 1 of the cell’s second-moment tensor: how elongated the cell is (near 0 for regular pentagons and heptagons). Paper: App. B.1.
  4. Pentagons / heptagons. A cell’s number of sides is its number of neighbors. By Euler’s formula, ∑(6 − sides) = 12 on any sphere; 4 or 8+ sides get darker shades.
  5. Admissible N. Each construction uses its admissible N nearest the slider value (odd N for the Fibonacci lattice, N = 10T + 2 for EqAreaIco).
  6. TZ-R is a numerically optimized variant seeded by TerracedZonal (Riemannian L-BFGS with defect moves), shown only as a reference and not ranked against the constructions. Paper: §5 and App. F.
  7. Complexity and accuracy. Complexity: time to generate the N points. Accuracy: growth of E − EBHS. TerracedZonal: Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 4.3 (under locality assumption (A)). Published constructions: complexity from their defining formulas; linear excess as measured in the paper’s Table 5 (App. F).
